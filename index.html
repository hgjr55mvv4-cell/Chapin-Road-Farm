Our current public project

Harbor Co-Listener

A song can move someone whose words they don't understand. Could AI recognize musical details more accurately, and learn what matters to a particular human without making up an emotional explanation? We're building an open-source listening instrument to investigate.

The Listening Room

Play audio you already have on your device. Mark meaningful moments, record another listener's perspective, and export JSON. No login, API charge or audio upload. The software does not claim to hear or feel the music.
