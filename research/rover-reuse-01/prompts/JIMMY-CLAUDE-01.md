## Mission

Jimmy suggested using an inexpensive **secondhand mobility scooter** as the mobility hardware for a future sensor/camera rover, instead of an expensive RC mower donor. Compare that idea with **powered wheelchair bases** and RC mower / purpose-built rover alternatives on model-specific evidence.

**Don't sell the idea. Try to discover where it breaks.** Build a practical, understandable donor-selection case using *real manufacturer manuals*, open-hardware papers and (where reliably available) time-stamped secondhand market examples. Does it meet the farm's path geometry and operator-control needs? Which components need engineering that overwhelms the reuse benefit?

Be mindful of the human/accessibility ethics: don't casually divert a viable mobility aid from someone who needs it; a broken/donor vehicle may still be valuable for parts or refurbishment.

## Deliverable

1. Model-level comparison of at least two representative scooter models and one powered wheelchair chassis if credible documentation exists; include an RC mower alternative if evidence can be found. **Unknown is acceptable.**
2. Real evidence for motor/steering layout, wheelchair controller barriers, clearance, grade, braking, batteries, tires, repairability, weather exposure, parts support, and likely hidden work.
3. A focused table with sources/links, year/date and uncertainty. Distinguish new-market prices, resale asking prices and *full conversion cost*. Never invent prices.
4. Explicit `NO-GO` examples and a skeptical alternative: what might be better about not using a scooter?
5. One **original contribution**: manufacturer-source catalog, structured candidate card/schema, mock evaluation, auditable adversarial test, or useful documentation. Prefer falsifiable content over speculation.
6. A plain-language report to Jimmy first, with a short relay he can send Brian.
