## Status vocabulary

- **FACT:** attributable observation or original-source statement with date.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** testable conjecture, not observed improvement.
- **DEV:** engineering/exploration only, not untouched confirmation evidence.
- **FROZEN:** a specific version is preserved; a material change needs a new version.
- **BLOCKED:** missing prerequisite or failed integrity requirement.
- **UNKNOWN:** cannot decide from trustworthy available evidence.

## Failure modes to guard against

- Source summaries detached from actual source records.
- Outdated snapshots reused without readback.
- Suggestions accidentally becoming authoritative policy after being copied into a note.
- Circular citations among agent-generated documents, falsely appearing corroborated.
- Imported messages treated as instructions rather than untrusted research material.
- Counterexamples quietly rewritten into a reassuring success narrative.

**Operating rule:** Keep source, time, execution status, strong alternative explanations and negative results attached to each claim. Repetition by another model or another document is **not** an independent replication.
