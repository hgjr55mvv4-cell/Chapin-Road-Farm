## 1. Your starting point — please investigate, don't just agree

Chapin Road Farm is a nonprofit community farm in Hampden, Massachusetts, with a human mission: growing food, sharing harvests, creating purpose and helping people of different abilities take part in the work, including people affected by multiple sclerosis.

We are exploring **green-teaming**: the constructive counterpart to AI red-teaming. Alongside finding faults and risks, we want to study when sustained human–AI collaboration actually benefits people in a real place—farm operations, equipment repairs, accessibility, music, practical knowledge, and open research. We want to document successes, disagreements, corrections, failures, added burden, consent, and better-than-AI alternatives.

The longer-term idea is a real-world setting for responsible human–AI cohabitation/integration. This is an **emerging research direction**, not proof of a field-qualified facility, AI consciousness, or measurable improvement already achieved.

Your job is **not to promote our story**. Investigate whether our methods and actual public work support what we say. Bring Jimmy an honest, intelligible outside assessment, and one useful new contribution we can examine.
