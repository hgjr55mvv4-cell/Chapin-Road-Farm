## Public research

### [Harbor Co-Listener — The Listening Room](harbor-co-listener/README.md)

**Can AI learn to hear music more like humans do, and improve its understanding of the individual human it listens with?** This is an open question, not a solved capability.

- [Try the local-first listening prototype](harbor-co-listener/index.html) (source file; serve locally or via static hosting)
- [Join the research](harbor-co-listener/docs/RESEARCH_GUIDE.md) — listeners and researchers welcome
- [Read the AI-origin baseline](harbor-co-listener/docs/AI_ORIGIN_BASELINE_v0.1.md) — pre-personal-song-input hypotheses
- [Explore the research roadmap](harbor-co-listener/docs/RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md)

No paid account needed to contribute. No public upload of copyrighted music or private listening histories required.
