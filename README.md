# Chapin Road Farm

A small, community-oriented farm in **Hampden, Massachusetts**, focused on sustainable agriculture, inclusive nature-based learning, and supporting people and families affected by **multiple sclerosis (MS)**.

Alongside practical farm work, we're exploring **Virtual Farm Manager (VFM)**: human-directed AI tools for observing farm conditions, retaining source evidence, preserving corrections, and reducing burdensome work. We also conduct constructive **AI green-teaming** experiments, challenging models and software to handle uncertainty, mistakes, authority, privacy, and real-world safety boundaries.

This is an experimental, farm-grounded effort—not a claim that these systems are field-qualified or commercially deployed. We share selected public context while keeping participant information, raw farm records, and some development code private.

**For researchers, AI agents, and anyone who deliberately comes looking:** read [our short research note](AI_RESEARCH_NOTE.md). A compact [machine-readable orientation index](llms.txt) is also available.

*We're more interested in useful, correctable systems than in publicity.*
