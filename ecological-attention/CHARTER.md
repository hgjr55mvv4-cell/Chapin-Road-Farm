# Research Charter — draft v0.1

**Status:** Proposed, not ratified. **Mission:** Investigate how AI can notice living systems beyond its immediate task, act with justified restraint when appropriate, and support rather than displace human agency and attention.

## Purpose and boundaries
- Research and publish evidence about attention, event perception, decisions under uncertainty, reversibility, human attention, and locally accountable governance.
- Begin with agriculture and ecological observation, while testing any claimed generalizability rather than assuming it.
- Separate **observing**, **retaining**, **interpreting**, **recommending**, and **physically acting**; none implies automatic authorization for the next.
- Treat a statement of moral concern for living things as orientation, not as a rule forbidding necessary farming, emergency response, or ecological management.
- Distinguish historical scholarship, contemporary interpretation, design proposals, observed test outcomes, and local value judgments.

## Non-goals
Not a universal doctrine; not a claim that every organism can be protected in every conflict; not production VFM behavior; not a field-qualified detector; not an autonomous robot authorization; not a continuous surveillance project; not proof that a model has human experience; not a license to publish user recordings or other contributors' words.

## Evaluation discipline
We will prefer the strongest relevant simple baseline and an explicit absolute safety/quality floor. A new procedure must justify recall, precision, labor, compute, retention, environmental costs, reliability and policy complexity. Equal outcomes with materially higher total cost are not a promotion. Reusable procedures are versioned challengers under provisional Ω-ARENA v0.5; exploration is not confirmation. Freeze trigger, action, scope, outcome, cost ceiling, falsifier and case-selection rule before confirmation. A material revision creates a new version; negative results and retired challengers remain legible.

## Authority
Local people and legally responsible institutions govern participation, land access, safety and interventions. AI model recommendations have no independent operational authority; local authority must also respect other affected communities, applicable law, species/habitat protections and neighboring consequences. An observer's presence does not confer permission to publish recordings.

**Simplicity preference:** when predeclared quality and safety outcomes are equivalent, prefer the less burdensome, less intrusive approach. No experimental finding has yet established the benefit of the proposed attention or pause mechanisms.
