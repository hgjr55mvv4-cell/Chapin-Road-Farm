# Governance, data and licensing — proposed

**Status: draft for maintainer, contributor and local-community review.** These are proposed research safeguards and are not yet a substitute for formal consent, legal review or field-specific governance.

## Who decides
A named, responsible human lead authorizes each study and any local land or equipment access. People whose privacy, property, livelihoods or legal rights may be affected require appropriate consultation/consent. Models may propose, criticize and document, but do not gain authority through consensus. Local decisions remain subject to law, safety duties and effects on neighboring communities.

## Minimum collection
- No default always-on cameras or audio recording; no observation of workers, visitors, neighbors or households without clear necessity and explicit authorization/consent.
- Prefer offline public-license test material, data minimization, short-lived processing, aggregation, opt-in capture and a genuine OFF setting. A detection event is not permission to retain its raw footage.
- Capture only the fields needed for a stated study. Define retention before collection; document deletion mechanisms and who can access anything retained.
- Do not publish identifying human information, health details, exact sensitive-species locations, precise participant movements, device identifiers, private farm operational records or raw recordings without specific rights clearance.
- A volunteer's participation in farm activities is not research consent. No separate model-training reuse without separate permission.
- No autonomous environmental treatment, pesticide use or other physical intervention is authorized by this project.

## Responsible publication
Every real dataset must have an admissible license and source/recording lineage, annotator/rights review, limitations, grouping for independence and release permissions. A link to a public file does not authorize republishing it. If data cannot be shared, disclose that limitation rather than silently replacing missing evidence.

## Licensing decision required before merge
This public draft does **not** claim all project materials are already open-licensed. Proposed choices for owner approval: MIT for original source code; CC BY 4.0 for original project-authored documentation. Third-party quotations, source editions, photographs, recordings, model-relayed outputs, participant contributions and datasets retain their separate rights and are excluded unless individually licensed/cleared. If these choices are accepted, add exact license files, a scope statement and required notices before presenting the merged project as reusable open source.

## Contributor protocol
Issue/PR submission should state: human purpose, source provenance, AI assistance disclosure if relevant, actual tests run, cases and leakage controls, risks, negative findings, approval boundaries and license/consent status. Criticism and corrective reviews are first-class contributions. Protect a quiet, accessible experience for participants, including the option not to be notified or monitored.
