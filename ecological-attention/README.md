# Open Ecological Intelligence
## Attention, restraint, and human agency in AI for living systems

**Status: FOUNDING DRAFT / DESIGN ONLY — October 25, 2026.** No detector, observation policy, ecological intervention, human-subjects study, or outcome claim is implemented or validated by this project.

This open research initiative began with an evening observation of small flying insects at Chapin Road Farm. Before their identity or ecological function was established, a farmer said: **“It’s all worth protecting.”** That is the moral starting point, not an executable decision rule.

**Research question:** Can AI systems help humans notice more of the living world, act with justified restraint, and retain direct attention and authority over their environment? We will compare proposals against credible simple baselines, make disagreement inspectable, and publish negative and null results.

### Research directions
1. **Attention:** events outside current task categories; the cost of false alarms and recording.
2. **Interpretation:** distinguish identity, consequence, value and authority uncertainty.
3. **Restraint:** whether time-bounded observation or deferral improves real decisions.
4. **Human agency:** whether AI supports rather than replaces human noticing.
5. **Plural dialogue:** locally accountable ecological judgments; no culture or model consensus as universal authority.
