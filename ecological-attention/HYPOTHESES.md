# Research hypotheses and separation of questions

All items are **PROPOSED / UNTESTED**, not confirmed. Change of an evaluation procedure requires a versioned candidate and prospective confirmation under the provisional Ω-ARENA discipline.

## H1 — Non-task ecological events
A bounded event-oriented observation mechanism detects more independently human-annotated non-task environmental events than a **strong conventional task-agnostic detection baseline** at an acceptable false-alert rate, review time, compute and retention cost. Not satisfied merely by outperforming a crop/weed label-only output.

See [EXP-001 design](experiments/EXP-001/DESIGN.md). Result: UNKNOWN.

## H2 — Justified deferral
When environmental decisions have low urgency, high irreversibility and material uncertainty, a deadline-bounded review procedure can reduce unnecessary irreversible actions relative to immediate action and simpler decision-theory baselines, **without unacceptable harmful delay**. Attention is held constant while testing this procedure. Result: UNKNOWN. EXP-002 NOT DESIGNED.

## H3 — Human attention and agency
Compared with a credible lower-interruption interface, a configurable quiet system may improve direct environmental noticing and perceived agency without increasing missed safety-critical events. Measurement must include physical/spatial displacement and energy burden, not just screen time. Human participation requires consent and appropriate review. Result: UNKNOWN. EXP-003 NOT DESIGNED.

## H4 — Observational restraint
An explicit, user-governed collection/retention schedule may preserve much of the relevant observation value with less privacy intrusion, energy use, operator burden and storage than always-on recording. A fully off mode is a valid baseline. Result: UNKNOWN. No field study authorized.

## Distinguish four uncertainties
1. **Identity:** What is the organism or event?
2. **Consequence:** What does it do, for whom, and over what timescale?
3. **Value:** What should matter in conflicts, beyond convenience or measured yield?
4. **Authority:** Who is empowered and responsible to decide?

Those uncertainties must not be collapsed into a single model confidence score.
