# Decision — AUDITORY-DATASET-ADMISSION-01

**Date:** October 23, 2026. **Decision:** REPAIR EVIDENCE FIRST; EXECUTABLE GATE CREATED; ORIGINAL DATASED RECORDINGS NOT EVALUATED; SCIENCE HOLD.

We accept Astra's user-relayed independent replication of the original ESC-10 negative result subject to normal authentication of the full source package. We do **not** accept the empirical claim that either ZCR or a nine-frequency prototype generalizes. The next bottleneck is neither a larger model nor a better classifier threshold: it is **the reliability and provenance of time-labeled target events and negatives**, with source-held-out grouping.

The gate is designed to ensure that failing or missing evidence is visible. It does not automatically inspect a waveform by ear or reconstruct an original recording's provenance. 14 unit tests were executed locally on synthetic fixtures before GitHub CI, exercising missing/invalid/unsafe cases and one deliberately constructed complete fixture.

An actual DataSED run **cannot proceed yet**: the complete Astra A–J report/ZIP, the nine candidate names, source-side lineage and file-level rights evidence, original selected WAV bytes and independent audio annotation review are not available in this conversation. The local container also cannot resolve Zenodo over the network. The public Zenodo record and published paper do substantiate the existence of time-label tables and the source-version size conflict; they do not resolve candidate lineage or independent event audibility.

**Next step:** If Astra's archive is delivered, preserve SHA-256 and exact candidate list. Admit a **small sample with independent human listening receipts and verified originals** before freezing a new cross-source evaluation. Do not infer negatives from empty time-label intervals. Consider a non-DataSED dataset if the file-lineage gap cannot be closed; publishing a HOLD is a valid result.

**VFM Acoustic Mechanic remains software-only research.** An audible recording-class tag, a temporal event, a component source, a mechanically confirmed diagnosis and safety-to-operate are five distinct states.
