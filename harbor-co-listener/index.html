The Listening Room

A song is a place we can meet. Bring music that means something to you. Mark what matters. Invite someone else to discover what you hear—without demanding they feel exactly the same thing.

Local-first · No AI required · No audio uploads

1. Bring your song

Choose a file already on your device. No audio leaves your browser.

You can write about a song without uploading a file.

2. Mark a moment

What sound, silence, turn, or memory makes this song important?

If audio is loaded, marking stores the current playback time. Otherwise, the note is untimed.

3. Another listener responds

This can be a human listener. Future experiments may add an optional AI assistant; this version does not simulate AI listening or claim to feel emotion.

Listening timeline

Research prototype, not a mind-reading tool. It does not measure brain activity, infer personal emotion from sound, or contact an AI provider. All notes exist only in this tab until you explicitly export a JSON file. The export does not include audio.
