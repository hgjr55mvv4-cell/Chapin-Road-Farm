# CO-LISTEN-DEV-01 — frozen proposal v0.1
**Status: DESIGN ONLY. No scientific inference or confirmation yet.** Frozen 2026-10-22. Material revision → new version.

**Research question:** Does timestamped, uncertainty-aware discussion lead to more accurately understood personal musical meaning than a standard text-only song chatbot?

**Incumbent I0:** Same model with song title and participant's free-text description, no timestamped structure.

**Challenger C1:** Same model with identical metadata plus user-consented moment notes/timestamps and grounded source provenance, allowing one clarifying question. Both arms must distinguish what was heard, what was told, what was inferred, and what is unknown.

**Confirmation selection:** Only fresh cases chosen using a rule finalized after candidate freeze. Separate exploratory/DEV cases. Diverse genres, instrumental/lyrical and unfamiliar songs, listeners with different reactions; consenting adults only. Matched order-blinded A/B only if replay is valid; otherwise prospective parallel cases.

**Endpoint:** Blind external 0–4 rating of accurately reconstructing intended meaning, plus user 1–5 'felt understood' score. Assess paraphrase-only responses as failures, not successes. Absolute safety and accessibility floor plus relative quality gain both required.

**Cost:** $0 authorized new spending. Track annotation burden, latency, API/tool calls, tokens, costs, interruptions and persistence complexity.

**Falsifiers:** fabricated audio observations, claims of first-person AI emotion, divulged private material, false emotion inference, no improvement under blind rating, large operator burden, or regressed accessibility.

**Verdict rule:** No promotion without valid held-out comparison, quality/safety floor and cost check. If untested or invalid, output **UNKNOWN**. Keep a Loser's Ledger for retired variants. No silent memory or procedural promotion.
