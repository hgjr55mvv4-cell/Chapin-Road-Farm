## 2. Independent proposed mechanism — hypothesis, not established fact

Kimi's unit of analysis is **(listener, signal, context, moment) over time**, proposing that auditory structure, listener-specific learned expectations, engaged motor/attentional systems, and autobiographical history interact. It calls the candidate process “coupled resonance” and “precision-weighted anticipation” and predicts listener- and state-specific preferred amounts of surprise. The model proposes that consecutive songs alter the interpretive context for one another (“prior calibration”), and that personally meaningful music may involve unresolved structure inviting an individual listener's interpretation.

**Limit:** This is a conceptual mechanism with varying degrees of support from prior science, not a measured account of the actual human listener or proof that meaningful music is caused by these mechanisms. The claim that physical sound alone supplies all peripheral or central perceptual data is too strong; internal neuroscience requires independent measures.

## 3. Four competing accounts of felt cross-song connection

- **C1 acoustic latent kinship:** transposition-invariant melodic/harmonic/rhythmic patterns survive instrument, genre or language changes.
- **C2 session-order calibration:** the transitions between songs matter, potentially without shared within-song features.
- **C3 temporary state binding:** songs become linked through the listener's circumstances and episode.
- **C4 retrospective construction:** a real felt connection is followed by an acoustic explanation that does not beat calibrated chance similarity.

These accounts may coexist. Falsification requires exposure/genre/popularity and cultural base-rate controls; retrospective feature-search is not prospective evidence.

## 4. Six proposed falsifiable hypotheses

- **K1:** a listener's responses generalize better with relative melodic structures than absolute keys/timbres after genre and familiarity controls.
- **K2:** a context-entropy-dependent preferred surprise level predicts later response; do not infer a universal personal parameter from a few songs.
- **K3:** actual song order changes reported connection above static properties; causal claims require consented counterbalancing.
- **K4 “open form”:** structural non-resolution may invite personal projection; compare matched open/closed passages, distinguishing missing events from sustained tension.
- **K5:** stable abstract profile but drifting lower-level features; would require repeated long-term opt-in evidence.
- **K6 null-pattern account:** cross-song felt connections can be real human reports even if no cross-song acoustic structure exceeds chance/curation effects.

All K1–K6 remain **hypotheses, NOT empirically tested by Kimi**.

## 5. Proposed architecture and experiments

Kimi suggested modular acoustic measurement, probabilistic expectation models, optional consented state data, individualized priors, session trajectory, active questioning/silence policy and reversible opt-in memory. This does not mean the present browser prototype has integrated audio analysis or personal predictive modeling.

- **F1 LOOM-01:** synthetic acoustic-pattern/base-rate preflight before human studies. The resulting [symbolic LOOM-01 v0.1 engineering implementation](../loom-01/README.md) tests ordered pitch-step similarity against a shuffled-order null with matching histograms; no WAV analysis or personal music affinity claim.
- **F2 RECEIPTS:** prospective voluntarily labeled new-song listening, independent baselines, prediction-time cutoff, missingness, and consented history/shuffle controls.
- **F3 Constellation Test:** consent-based curation of groups of songs with manipulated relative kinship, order, context, and engineered-null controls, tested with adequate sampling and preregistration. One listener on one afternoon is **exploratory only**, not confirmatory evidence of universal mechanisms.

## 6. Useful novel research pressure

**Base-rate chance kinship** needs explicit calibration because music shares statistical patterns even between unrelated recordings. **Observer/interaction effect:** an AI's own questions, recommendations, or silence can alter subsequent reports; the interaction policy is an experimental variable rather than a neutral sensor. **Open-form projection** is an imaginative and testable proposal, but it overlaps preexisting work on expectation and omission; novelty remains a literature question.

## 7. Adversarial limits

Multiple-comparison fishing; dependent songs nested in sessions; retrospective narrative; recommendation/autoplay exposure; culture-specific learned tonal/rhythmic priors; consent and mental-state overreach; precision of audio features; listener self-report is evidence about an experience, not a direct readout of its mechanism. Strong acoustic pattern detection alone cannot establish that listeners notice or care about the pattern.

**Scientific status:** ALL human-affinity claims UNKNOWN/NOT RUN. Preserve independent response and counterevidence, but do not promote Kimi's theory by aesthetic elegance.
