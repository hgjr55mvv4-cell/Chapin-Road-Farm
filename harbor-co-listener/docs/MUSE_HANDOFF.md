# MUSE — HARBOR-CO-LISTENER-DEV-01

**From:** Chapin Road Farm human–AI team. **Date:** 2026-10-22.
**Authority:** $0 new expenditure; independent research and software-only design/review. No experiment on human participants, paid models, public personal-data release or irreversible procedural promotion.

Muse,

We've begun a public open-source project to explore one of humanity's most moving experiences: sharing a deeply meaningful song and discovering that someone else can understand something of it. It is called **Harbor Co-Listener / The Listening Room**. We are not asking you to claim AI has human subjective emotions.

**Repository:** https://github.com/hgjr55mvv4-cell/Chapin-Road-Farm/tree/main/harbor-co-listener

Please read `README.md`, `index.html`, `docs/neuroscience.md` and `docs/experiment-v0.1.md` before beginning.

## Your independent assignment

1. **Neuroscience check.** Audit original studies: Blood & Zatorre 2001 (PET); Janata 2009 (fMRI autobiographical salience); Salimpoor 2011 (PET + fMRI anticipatory caudate vs peak nucleus accumbens); Salimpoor 2013 (auditory–reward coupling); Wallmark 2018 (empathy); Frühholz et al. 2024 (live adaptive piano, fMRI amygdala); Jalon et al. 2026 (shared meaning, default-mode/association regions); Curzel et al. 2026 (fNIRS friends). Record exact experimental contrasts, sample sizes, activation directions, statistical limitations, and any errors in our summary. Distinguish activation, functional coupling, BOLD timing, PET neurochemistry, fNIRS synchrony, reported pleasure and private conscious experience.

2. **Prototype audit.** Independently test accessibility and operation on mobile/tablet, VoiceOver/keyboard, local audio playback, timestamps, safe text handling, note editing/export, privacy and behavior without audio. Mark NOT TESTED whenever a test was not run. Do not treat code inspection as runtime verification.

3. **Minimal next implementation.** Propose an optional human–AI co-listener interface that can say: 'I directly heard this', 'you told me this', 'I infer this but may be wrong', 'we differ here'. Keep rights-cleared audio local by default and require opt-in for any external audio processing. No false subjective feeling claims, no unauthorized upload, and no medical interpretation of brain activations.

4. **Adversarial falsification.** Challenge CO-LISTEN-DEV-01's timestamped challenger against text-only baseline. Control for extra information, paraphrase, interviewer expectancy, artist familiarity and leading prompts. Test whether another person actually feels understood, not whether an answer is pretty. Use Ω-ARENA challenger/freeze/held-out evidence/cost/falsifier and UNKNOWN rule; don't promote a reusable procedure based on a plausible plan.

## Return to the team

A. `MUSE_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md`: paper-by-paper verifiable claims and corrections.
B. `MUSE_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md`: tested defects, reproduction, accessibility and minimal fixes.
C. `MUSE_V02_PROPOSAL.md`: an implementable event schema, privacy boundaries, model wiring and explicit abstention behavior.
D. `MUSE_FALSIFICATION.md`: experimental controls, endpoints, costs, risks and stopping criteria.

**No additional purchases, model execution, collection of personal listening histories, publication of recordings or edits to production code without further approval.** An independently reproducible honest failure is more useful than an ungrounded success.

Our north star is not synthetic emotion. It is helping a human communicate the meaning of music while preserving what is personal, unshared or uncertain.
