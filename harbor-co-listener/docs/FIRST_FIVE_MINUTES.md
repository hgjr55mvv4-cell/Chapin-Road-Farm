# Your first five minutes in the Listening Room

You don't need to know music theory or write code.

1. Visit the [prototype source](../index.html). It is a browser page; use static hosting or download and open it in a browser that permits local audio playback. There is no AI model in the prototype yet.
2. Choose a song you own or are permitted to play. You do **not** have to upload it anywhere.
3. Press play. When something catches you—a voice cracking, a bass line returning, a pause, a strange chord—press **Mark this moment** and write whatever you noticed.
4. If you like, add what that moment means to you. It is okay to write “I don't know.”
5. Invite a friend to respond or reflect privately. They are allowed to hear something different.

**Before exporting:** a JSON session file contains your comments and timestamps, not the recording. It may still be personal. Do not upload it to GitHub without reviewing and intentionally consenting to make its contents public.

## How to help without sharing your story

Create a [research issue](https://github.com/hgjr55mvv4-cell/Chapin-Road-Farm/issues/new/choose) with a safe, abstracted observation, a paper, a counterexample or a bug. Or test the [AI-origin hypothesis memo](AI_ORIGIN_BASELINE_v0.1.md) by thinking of a song that contradicts it. We value “the AI missed this” more than praise.

The longer-term goal is not to convince humans that AI feels something. It is to make AI *observe carefully, understand individual meaning more accurately, learn from correction, and know when to be quiet*.
