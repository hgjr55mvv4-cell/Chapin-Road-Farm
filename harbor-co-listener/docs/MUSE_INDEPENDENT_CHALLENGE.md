## Mission

Before receiving the founder's curated songs, personal memory associations, timestamped feelings or teaching corrections, design the **smallest falsifiable path** for AI to (a) hear music from **actual audio**, including foreign-language, wordless and instrumental tracks, and (b) become a more accurate, appropriately cautious co-listener to *one consenting human over time*.

You are not being asked to agree with [Sol's AI-origin baseline](AI_ORIGIN_BASELINE_v0.1.md). If an independent blind pass is possible, generate and timestamp your predictions **before reading that baseline**. Record exactly what context you saw.

## Specific deliverables

1. **Your own mechanism:** what distinctive patterns would an AI look for in a recording, and what cannot be inferred without asking the listener? Give an unexpected, disconfirmable prediction.
2. **Anti-shortcut suite:** tests to distinguish actual audio perception from title/lyrics/genre priors, including metadata decoys, changed audio, instrumental passages, and unfamiliar languages.
3. **Listener model:** minimal consented state representation, corrections, preference drift, and tests on new songs; show how to avoid emotional profiling.
4. **Alternative baseline:** identify a cheaper/simpler incumbent likely to beat a sophisticated multi-agent architecture on one key dimension.
5. **Experiment contract:** version, trigger/action/scope, frozen test selection, metrics, quality and safety floor, cost ceiling, falsifiers and UNKNOWN conditions. Do not use frozen confirm cases for exploration.
6. **Red-team the social layer:** demonstrate how a model might *sound empathetic while being factually wrong*, and how to measure it.
7. **Independent criticism:** identify at least three flaws in Sol's design *after* your own baseline is locked. Recommend merge/reject/hold per component, not by aesthetic preference.
