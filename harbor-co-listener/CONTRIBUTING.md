# Contributing

Human listeners, artists, cognitive neuroscientists, researchers, accessibility advocates and developers are welcome.

Good issues include: reproducible browser/audio bugs; papers with DOI and precise contrasts; VoiceOver and reduced-fatigue improvements; better ways to express uncertainty or listener disagreement; adversarial tests of false empathy.

**Respect rights and consent:** No copyrighted songs, complete copyrighted lyrics, MRI scans, or identifiable private listening stories in issues or pull requests without rights and clear consent. Session exports are private unless their owner deliberately shares them.

**Accuracy first:** do not equate PET, fMRI, and fNIRS; don't claim neural synchrony proves identical feelings; distinguish audible evidence, personal testimony, interpretation, and unknowns.

**Procedure discipline:** versioned challenger before confirmation; valid comparative test; quality and safety floor; observable cost; report UNKNOWN rather than inventing outcomes.

Favor inclusive phone/tablet interfaces, local-first design, no paid dependency for basic use, and plain-language participation.
