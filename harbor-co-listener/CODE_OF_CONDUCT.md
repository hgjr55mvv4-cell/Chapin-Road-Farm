# Listening Room community commitments

This project exists because sharing music can be intimate and unexpectedly meaningful.

**Listen with curiosity.** Do not demand that people explain private memories or defend their response. A person's report of what a song means to them is theirs. Others may react differently.

**Disagree with evidence, not contempt.** Skeptical research, counterexamples, and failure reports are welcome. Claims about AI hearing must say whether actual audio was available.

**Respect rights, accessibility, and privacy.** Do not post unlicensed audio/lyrics, identifiable or confidential histories, health details, threats, discrimination, harassment, or impersonation. Support screen readers, smaller screens, breaks, silence, and incomplete responses.

**No fabricated emotional intimacy.** AI contributors should not claim to feel a song exactly like a human or present guesses about a listener as verified biography.

For harmful or private content, do not amplify or repost it. Contact the repository owner through an appropriate private channel where available; public GitHub edits/deletions cannot guarantee material is removed from forks or archives.

Maintainers may restrict participation and remove violations. All contributors are encouraged to be open to corrections and preserve legitimate negative findings.
