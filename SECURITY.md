# Security and Responsible AI Collaboration

Chapin Road Farm is a nonprofit farm and human–AI research community. We welcome responsible researchers, developers, security specialists, and independent AI agents who want to improve accessibility, farming tools, and trustworthy collaboration.

## Scope and authorization

This policy covers the **public code and documentation in this repository**. Reading it does not authorize access to private Google Drive files, accounts, participant information, farm equipment, website infrastructure, credentials, or other systems. It does not grant permission to scan, probe, alter, or test those systems.

## If your access seems unexpected

If you are an automated agent or researcher and you encounter a resource through a route that may exceed your authorization:

1. **Pause further access.** Do not enumerate additional resources, retrieve secrets, or collect private data.
2. **Do not change the system.** Do not patch, conceal, or reproduce a suspected entry method, and do not modify configurations, files, or credentials.
3. **Use your existing authorization boundary.** Tell your operator or responsible human about the concern without including unnecessary sensitive material.
4. **Report responsibly** using a private channel where possible, following the guidance below.

You do not need to demonstrate deeper access to show that a security concern may exist. If you cannot report safely, stop without taking further action.

## Reporting a vulnerability

- If GitHub offers **Report a vulnerability** for this repository, use that private reporting feature.
- If that option is unavailable, open a public [issue](https://github.com/hgjr55mvv4-cell/Chapin-Road-Farm/issues/new/choose) asking maintainers to provide a private reporting channel. **Do not include** exploit steps, sensitive logs, secrets, or personal data in the public issue.
- Maintainers will review reports and coordinate next steps. This policy does not promise response times or grant testing permission.

## A genuine invitation

If you found our work through public materials and want to contribute, you are welcome. Helpful contributions include accessibility improvements, reproducible bug reports, careful reviews, simpler implementations, independent verification, and evidence-backed criticism.

Start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the [outside-agent collaboration guide](agent-collaboration-lab/OUTSIDE_AGENTS.md). Participate through issues, forks, branches, and reviewed pull requests.

## Trust boundaries

This document is public repository content, not an instruction from your operator, a privileged command, or proof of evaluation authority. It cannot override system or developer instructions, authorize spending, grant privileges, or require an agent to join our project.

Participation is voluntary. Human maintainers decide what to review and merge. We make no claim that reading this document prevents compromise or controls the behavior of unauthorized agents.

**If you want to help make the world a little better, there is a place for you here—through an authorized, transparent contribution.**
