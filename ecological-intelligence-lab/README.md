# Ecological Intelligence Lab (EIL)

**Status:** DESIGN ONLY / DRAFT — NOT RUN, NOT CONFIRMED, NOT A RESEARCH LAUNCH.
**Founding issue:** [#88](https://github.com/hgjr55mvv4-cell/Chapin-Road-Farm/issues/88) (EIL-FOUNDING-01).
**Authority:** Independent research, design, and draft-PR preparation only. No merge, deployment, scored experiment, field intervention, farm-procedure change, or spending is authorized by this scaffold.
**Budget:** $0 additional.

## What this lab studies

Whether a system can recognize ecological relationships, anticipate consequences, handle uncertainty, and choose well among **observation, inquiry, intervention, and restraint** — and whether independent human–AI collaboration adds anything beyond a strong single-agent baseline on such decisions.

These are scientific hypotheses, not established findings. Philosophy ("It's all worth protecting") motivates the inquiry; only evidence can justify its conclusions.

## What this lab is not

- **Not a new kind of intelligence.** The founding independent review (Round 01, Muse) recommends **rejecting "ecological intelligence" as a unitary construct** and adopting the narrower working object in `FOUNDING_QUESTIONS.md`: *ecologically situated decision competence (ESDC)* — competence measured against a human-specified objective on specified socio-ecological scenarios. An agent can score highly on ESDC while serving an extractive objective; that is high competence, not a failure of the construct. Building "beneficial outcome" into the definition of the capacity would make every test circular.
- **Not a field program.** No field interventions, autonomous robotics, model training, paid inference, environmental manipulation, or changes to farm operating procedures. Track EIL-04 (real-world ecological validity) is **PARKED** until a field protocol, a consent framework, and a human decision-maker for every consequential step exist.
- **Not a second collaboration lab.** Collective method lives in the [Agent Collaboration Lab](../agent-collaboration-lab/README.md) and its protocol; EIL contributes vignettes and misconception cases, not a parallel method.
- **Not a claim about machine feeling.** Nothing here implies that a model possesses ecological concern, subjective experience, or moral agency because it produces ecologically appropriate language. Language and understanding are separated by design (style-only controls in the experiment registry).

## Evidence discipline (inherited, not duplicated)

- Evidence states: `IDEA`, `DESIGN`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `TESTED`, `CONFIRMED`, `UNKNOWN` (see repository `README.md`).
- DEV and untouched confirmation evidence are strictly separate; no frozen confirmation material is used for design tuning.
- Every complex method is compared against a **strong simple baseline** at equal cost, and human coordination burden counts as a cost (repository `AGENTS.md`).
- Freeze an experiment's contract (cases, baseline, metric, floors, falsifier) before any run; keep exploration separate from confirmation (Ω-ARENA discipline).
- If a result does not beat the simpler incumbent, prefer the incumbent. If a construct cannot be operationalized without circularity, retire it — that is a successful outcome, not a failure.
- The founding insect observation is **UNKNOWN** and unexamined in the founding review; it is not experimental evidence for anything until it has its own provenance record.
